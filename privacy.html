IL IMPACT Lab App – privacy

The IMPACT Lab App is used by the members of the IMPACT Lab, Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Roorkee, for bills, attendance, leave and lab work.

Sign in with Google

When you sign in with Google, the app receives only your name, e-mail address and Google profile photo. They are used to find or create your account in the app and are not shared with anybody outside the lab.

What the app keeps

Your account, the bills you make, your attendance marks (time, place and the selfie of the first mark of the day), leave applications and lab work are stored in the lab's own Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive of IMPACT Lab). Daily attendance details are deleted after 90 days; monthly totals are kept. Only the lab admin, the attendance manager and the people the app shows them to can see them.
